## Limitations and known issues

These short probes validate the dispatch start/continue lifecycle with current sign-ins. They do not establish expired-credential refresh for Grok ([#421](https://github.com/conn-castle/agent-layer/issues/421)) or the cause of prior unclassified shutdown failures ([#430](https://github.com/conn-castle/agent-layer/issues/430)); both remain open. They do not exercise the GUI-client `PATH` launcher fix (#513), which is covered by its automated tests.

Raw probe reports and the options output are retained locally in `.agent-layer/tmp/release-v1.2.0/`; invocation records live under `.agent-layer/tmp/runs/<invocation_id>/`. These are local evidence locators, not uploaded artifacts. No signing, notarization, hosted certification, or publication is claimed by these provider probes.
